## Sample-count sensitivity (M) of the robustness ranking

Returned solutions re-evaluated offline with M in {5, 10, 20, 40} against a 100,000-sample
Monte-Carlo reference:

| M | Kendall tau (mean) | Kendall tau (min) | Mean abs. error |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 0.988 | 0.333 | 0.008 |
| 10 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.006 |
| 20 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.004 |
| 40 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.003 |

For every M >= 10 the ranking matches the reference exactly on every run; M*(D) in
[18, 40] lies well inside this invariant regime.

# Results

Per-problem result tables and post-hoc audits accompanying the paper
"Two-Stage Robust Differential Evolution for Multimodal Optimization".
All numbers are recomputed from the frozen experimental data and match the
paper (12-page self-contained version) exactly.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `robbench_per_problem.md` | RobF1 (mean +/- std, 10 runs) on all 20 RobBench problems, nine algorithms |
| `cec2026_per_problem.md` | CEC2026 competition score on all 16 problems at D = 2/5/10/20, nine algorithms |
| `ablation.md` | Group-level RobF1 for the full TSRDE and three ablated variants |
| `nsol_sensitivity.md` | RobF1 vs. n_sol sweep (1400 runs) and the Delta-n tolerance curve |
| `audits.md` | Near-optimality audit, robustness-ordering consistency check, sample-count sensitivity |

Provenance: numbers are generated by `scripts/gen_github_results.py` from the
frozen raw results (`results/`, `results_final/`) and the aggregate outputs
(`paper_prepare/figures/*.json`) of the paper repository. The extended version
of the paper (main text + appendix) is `../TSRDE_extended_version.pdf`.
